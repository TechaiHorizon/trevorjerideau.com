# Tinashe and Busta Rhymes credit sources

Updated November 20, 2026. This source record supports the two expanded artist entries in “Culture, on Record.” Trevor Jerideau confirmed his roles directly: album producer and A&R on Tinashe’s RCA releases, and A&R on Busta Rhymes’s Genesis and It Ain’t Safe No More... Public sources support release details and achievements, not a claim that each cited award was personally given to Trevor.

## Tinashe — RCA releases only

The included releases are Aquarius (2014), Nightride (2016) and Joyride (2018). Independent mixtapes, including Amethyst, and post-RCA albums are excluded. Nightride is described as a project because contemporary label coverage called it a mixtape, while the current Wikipedia discography classifies it as a studio album. [1] [2] [3] [4] [5]

Aquarius reached No. 17 on the Billboard 200, No. 2 on Top R&B Albums and No. 3 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums. The album ranked No. 3 on Billboard’s and Rolling Stone’s 2014 R&B year-end lists and appeared on Pitchfork’s and Spin’s album lists. Its singles include 2 On, Pretend and All Hands on Deck. [2] [6]

The RIAA 4× Platinum certification belongs to the single 2 On, not the Aquarius album. The certification date is June 16, 2025. The single reached No. 24 on the Hot 100 and spent four weeks at No. 1 on Rhythmic Airplay. It received a 2014 Soul Train Best Dance Performance nomination and a 2015 BMI Most Performed R&B/Hip-Hop Songs award. All Hands on Deck has Gold certifications in New Zealand and Australia; the Australian entry identifies the Iggy Azalea remix. No U.S. Gold or Platinum certification for Aquarius is claimed. [7] [8] [9] [10]

Tinashe’s artist-level recognition in the RCA period includes 2015 Best New Artist nominations at the BET Awards and Soul Train Awards, and a 2015 YouTube Music Awards honor. These are described as artist recognition, not as awards given to Aquarius or Trevor. [1] [11] [12] [13]

Nightride reached No. 8 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums and No. 89 on the Billboard 200. It ranked No. 16 on Rolling Stone’s 20 Best R&B Albums of 2016 and No. 14 on Fuse’s Best 20 Albums of 2016. Its track list includes Company, Party Favors and Ride of Your Life. Sony’s later Joyride announcement also confirms Nightride’s year-end recognition. [3] [5]

Joyride reached No. 58 on the Billboard 200 and No. 6 on the UK R&B Albums chart. Sony’s official announcement confirms No Drama with Offset, Faded Love with Future, Me So Bad with Ty Dolla $ign and French Montana, and Stuck with Me with Little Dragon. No unverified certification or album award is added. [4] [5]

## Busta Rhymes — two credited albums

Genesis (2001) received RIAA U.S. Platinum certification on March 11, 2002. It reached No. 7 on the Billboard 200 and No. 2 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums. Break Ya Neck reached No. 26 on the Hot 100. The later single/remix Pass the Courvoisier Part II reached No. 11 on the Hot 100 and received a 2003 Grammy nomination for Best Rap Performance by a Duo or Group. Part II belongs to the album’s single campaign and later pressings; it is not described as the original album track. Its nomination is a single/performance honor, not a Genesis album nomination or a personal nomination for Trevor Jerideau. [14] [15] [16] [17] [18] [19]

It Ain’t Safe No More... (2002) received RIAA U.S. Gold certification on January 6, 2003. It reached No. 43 on the Billboard 200 and No. 10 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums. I Know What You Want, with Mariah Carey and Flipmode Squad, reached No. 3 on the Hot 100. Make It Clap, featuring Spliff Star, reached No. 46; Sean Paul appeared on the remix. No unverified Grammy accolade is claimed. [16] [17] [18] [20] [21]
