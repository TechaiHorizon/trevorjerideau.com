# Swizz Beatz / Full Surface — portfolio sources

Trevor Jerideau personally confirms that he secured a multimillion-dollar deal for Swizz Beatz’s Full Surface Records at Clive Davis’s newly formed J Records, one of its early hip-hop label partnerships. This personal deal credit and value category come from Trevor. No exact dollar amount, deal date, label-founding claim or album-specific creative role has been added.

## J Records and RCA Music Group context

The requested Full Surface Wikipedia page provides the label history and discography. Contemporary AllHipHop coverage dated January 28, 2003 describes Full Surface’s distribution through J Records, lists Cassidy, Yung Wun and Mashonda among its early roster, and reports J Records’ combination with RCA in RCA Music Group. The website therefore identifies the J Records/RCA Music Group era without incorrectly changing the documented release imprint of the albums to RCA. [1] [2]

The Full Surface partnership card credits Trevor’s deal. The five subsequent cards are explicitly labeled **Full Surface catalog** achievements. Their recording charts, certifications and nominations belong to the artists and recordings, not personal awards to Trevor or unconfirmed producer/A&R credits.

## Cassidy — Split Personality, 2004

The Full Surface/J Records debut reached No. 2 on the Billboard 200 and No. 1 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums. Its verified U.S. RIAA certification is **Gold**, not Platinum. The official certification entry is dated April 19, 2004. “Hotel” reached No. 4 on the Hot 100, No. 6 on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs and No. 3 on the UK Singles Chart. “Get No Better,” featuring Mashonda, reached No. 82 on the Hot 100. Album guests also include Snoop Dogg, Styles P and Jadakiss. [3] [4] [5]

Billboard’s June 2005 coverage corroborates “Hotel”’s No. 4 Hot 100 peak. An inaccessible archived Vibe reference did not establish a reported “Hotel” nomination, so no such nomination is claimed. [6]

## Yung Wun — The Dirtiest Thirstiest, 2004

The Full Surface/J Records album reached No. 50 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums, No. 24 on Top Rap Albums and No. 11 on Heatseekers Albums. “Tear It Up,” featuring DMX, Lil’ Flip and David Banner, reached No. 76 on the Hot 100 and No. 39 on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs. Billboard independently supports the Hot 100 peak. “Walk It, Talk It,” featuring David Banner, reached No. 97 on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs. Cassidy also appears on the album. No supported RIAA certification or Grammy claim was established. [7] [8] [9]

The song and album sources disagree on single ordering, so “Tear It Up” is not described as the first or second single.

## Cassidy — I’m a Hustla, 2005

The Full Surface/J Records album debuted at No. 5 on the Billboard 200 and reached No. 2 on both Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums and Top Rap Albums. The Swizz Beatz-produced title track reached No. 34 on the Hot 100 and No. 5 on Hot Rap Songs. It received a **2005 Vibe Award nomination for Street Anthem**, not a win. Other album highlights include “B-Boy Stance” and guests Nas, Mario, Lil Wayne, Fabolous and Mary J. Blige. [10] [11] [12]

RIAA lists “I’M A HUSTLA” as a **Gold digital single**, dated January 25, 2006, and a **Platinum mastertone**, dated June 14, 2006. These are song-format certifications, not Gold/Platinum certifications for the album. The portfolio spells out the mastertone/ringtone distinction. No supported Grammy claim is made. [13]

## Mashonda — January Joy, 2005

The Full Surface/J Records debut features Kanye West, Snoop Dogg, Jadakiss, Nas and Raphael Saadiq. “Back of da Club” reached No. 86 on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs and “Blackout” reached No. 96. Swizz Beatz is listed as executive producer and as the producer of “Back of da Club”; those are his credits, not newly assigned roles for Trevor. [14] [15]

The sources disagree on the release month, so only 2005 is shown. No supported album-chart peak, Gold/Platinum certification or awards claim is added.

## Cassidy — B.A.R.S. The Barry Adrian Reese Story, 2007

The Full Surface/J Records release reached No. 10 on the Billboard 200, No. 3 on Top R&B/Hip-Hop Albums and No. 2 on Top Rap Albums. Billboard corroborates its No. 10 debut. “My Drink n My 2 Step,” featuring Swizz Beatz, reached No. 33 on the Hot 100 and No. 11 on Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs. “Innocent Man (Misunderstood),” featuring Mark Morrison, reached No. 3 on **Bubbling Under Hot 100 Singles**, not No. 3 on the Hot 100. Album guests include John Legend, Eve and Bone Thugs-n-Harmony. No supported Grammy or RIAA certification claim is made. [16] [17] [18] [19]

## Releases outside the requested label period

*Swizz Beatz Presents G.H.E.T.T.O. Stories* was investigated but excluded from the public cards because it was a Full Surface/DreamWorks release, not a J Records release. *Strength & Loyalty* is Full Surface/Interscope; *One Man Band Man* is Full Surface/Universal Motown. These do not belong to the J Records/RCA catalog cards. Announced but unreleased projects and unrelated Swizz Beatz production credits are also excluded. Guest appearances by Eve or Bone Thugs-n-Harmony on an included Cassidy album remain legitimate in-scope recording context. [20] [21] [22]
